## English

Before You Speak (开口前) is AI English speaking practice for Chinese-speaking professionals working abroad. Two AI colleagues with real accents act out the conversation you are about to have, such as a 1:1 with your manager, a meeting or small talk, and stop at your turn. Afterwards you get at most two corrections, explained in Chinese, which go into a review deck you practise by speaking. The app's interface and explanations are in Chinese.
